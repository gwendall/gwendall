# Hi, I'm Gwendall 👋

I make AI characters — the kind you care about, not the kind you give tasks to.

For the past 15 years, I've built games, avatars, cameras, and 3D worlds. I keep
coming back to the same question: how do you give digital things an identity of
their own?

## Start here

- 🎭 [**Kami**](https://kami.bot) — AI characters with a life of their own, for
  group chats, apps, robots, and worlds.
- 🌐 [**Domani**](https://domani.run) — domain and email infrastructure built for
  humans and AI agents.
- ✅ [**Tasq**](https://tasq.run) — local-first commitment coordination for
  humans, agents, and runtimes.

## Open source
